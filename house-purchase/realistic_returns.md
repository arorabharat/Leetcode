# Realistic Investment Returns for a Passive Investor

To effectively use the Buy vs Rent Simulator, we need to establish a realistic, "lazy" investment return target. This requires looking at historical averages for a passive Indian investor over a 20-year horizon and factoring in **taxes**. 

Here is what you can effortlessly achieve by just setting up a disciplined auto-SIP and rarely checking it:

## 1. The Asset Classes (Post-Tax Expectations)

*   **Fixed Deposits / Debt (Safe Money):** FDs generally give ~6.5% to 7%. If you are in the 30% tax bracket, your real post-tax return is only about **4.5% to 5%**.
*   **Sensex / Nifty 50 (Indian Equity):** The Indian market historically delivers 12% to 14% over 20-year periods. After factoring in the 12.5% Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax, your post-tax return is realistically **10.5% to 12%**.
*   **Nasdaq 100 (US Tech / Diversification):** Historically delivers 12% to 15% in Rupee terms (because the Rupee historically depreciates against the Dollar by ~3% a year, boosting INR returns). Even after taxation, you can comfortably expect **11% to 13%** post-tax.

## 2. The "Passive Investor" Portfolios

Depending on how you split your SIP, here is your easy target:

> [!NOTE]
> **Scenario A: The Cautious Investor (50% FD / 50% Nifty)**
> *   You put half your money in the bank and half in the stock market.
> *   *Expected Post-Tax Return:* **~8.0%**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Scenario B: The Balanced "Fire & Forget" (30% FD / 50% Nifty / 20% Nasdaq)**
> *   You keep a safe emergency cushion but let the majority ride the growth of India and US tech.
> *   *Expected Post-Tax Return:* **~9.5% to 10.0%**

> [!TIP]
> **Scenario C: The Aggressive Passive (10% FD / 60% Nifty / 30% Nasdaq)**
> *   You only keep 10% in debt for emergencies and go heavy into equities for the full 20 years. 
> *   *Expected Post-Tax Return:* **~11.0% to 11.5%**

## The Conclusion for the Simulator

If you want an **"easy, highly probable"** target for Guy 2 (the Renter) in the Java simulator, **9.5% to 10%** is the golden number. 

If we plug a **10%** investment return back into the *"Appreciation + 3%"* rule from our matrix analysis:
**For the Buyer to beat a 10% passive stock portfolio, the apartment *must* appreciate at 7% or higher consistently for 20 years.** If the apartment only appreciates at 6%, the passive, disciplined renter wins!
