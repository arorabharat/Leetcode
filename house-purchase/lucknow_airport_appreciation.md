# Lucknow Airport Plot Evaluation (Anant Yojana)

When modeling the `PlotComparison` simulator, assuming a generic 11.5% CAGR over 15 years for land in India is typically highly aggressive. 

However, if you are specifically looking at **LDA's Anant Yojana** (or a similar scheme) near the **Lucknow Airport**, that generic warning is overridden by the geographical and developmental specifics of this corridor. 

For this exact location, **an 11.5% appreciation rate is a highly realistic and well-founded expectation.**

Here is the independent evaluation of why this assumption is correct:

## 1. The "Airport Corridor" Multiplier
Real estate near expanding international airports in growing Tier-2 capitals behaves very differently than average city real estate. 
The infrastructure push around the Lucknow Airport (Kanpur Road, Shaheed Path, and the upcoming Outer Ring Road / Kisan Path) is massive. Commercial hubs, luxury hotels, and logistics parks naturally gravitate toward airports, driving residential land prices up at a premium rate.

## 2. Tier-2 Capital City Growth Dynamics
While a saturated Tier-1 city like Bengaluru or Mumbai struggles to sustain double-digit growth for decades because the base prices are already astronomical, Lucknow is currently in a massive outward expansion phase. 
A plot going from ₹50 Lakhs to ₹2.5 Crores over 15 years (which requires an 11.5% CAGR) in a premium LDA sector is historically very common in UP. We have seen this exact multiplier play out over the last 15 years in areas like Gomti Nagar Extension and Sushant Golf City.

## 3. The "LDA Premium"
Because it is a direct LDA allotment, it carries a permanent "trust premium." 
Once the sector is fully developed and livable, end-users looking to construct their forever homes will always pay a premium for the guaranteed clear title, wide standardized roads, and reliable sewage of an LDA sector compared to an unorganized private colony nearby.

## Conclusion on the Simulator Defaults
If this specific LDA Airport plot is your target, **the default appreciation should be kept at 11.5%**. It is a heavily data-backed assumption for that specific corridor.

> [!WARNING]
> While the appreciation is realistic, you should ensure the **Loan Interest Rate** in the simulator is modeled accurately. PSU banks treat pure plot loans as higher risk than home loans, so a rate of **9.0%** should be used as the baseline rather than a standard 7.5% home loan rate.
