# PO Centralization

## Summary

You worked on a proposal to centralize purchase-order identification/allocation logic across teams, reducing overlapping approaches and improving coordination in the supply-chain ecosystem.

## Documented stakeholder story

While preparing metrics to present the proposal to leadership, a team declined to help and the interaction reflected prior organizational friction. You sought context from your manager and skip-level, then used an existing relationship with an engineer on that team to obtain the necessary metrics. Leadership was convinced by the analysis and approved the PO Centralization proposal; the previously reluctant team later returned and accepted the proposal.

## Relationship to DoR

Another story says the DoR project exposed overlapping PO-identification logic across teams and built on the centralization proposal. It describes investigation of the Inventory Adjustment team's process and sharing analysis tools and design documents.

## Your documented contribution

- Gathered cross-team metrics and completed a leadership-facing analysis.
- Built/maintained working relationships to unblock information gathering.
- Connected PO centralization findings to DoR integration needs and shared tools/design context across teams.

## Limits

The repository does not explain the final centralized system architecture, implementation owner, launch status, or measurable reduction in conflicts. The story clearly supports proposal/leadership alignment and organizational collaboration, but not necessarily end-to-end implementation ownership.
