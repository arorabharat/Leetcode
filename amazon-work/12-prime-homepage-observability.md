# Prime homepage observability

## Summary

You worked to make technical health and feature performance observable for Prime experiences rendered by a high-traffic Tier-1 service on the Amazon homepage and checkout surfaces.

## Problem

The service-owning team had declined custom metrics because of infrastructure cost at high traffic. Prime teams were relying on delayed weekly business reports and manually investigating metric drops, clicks, and presentation issues. One story estimates 3–4 engineering days of manual work per query.

## Your documented contribution

- Partnered with a PM and reviewed prior on-call tickets to quantify the operational cost.
- Negotiated a constrained set of 15–20 technical metrics intended to cover most investigation needs without an unacceptable cost increase.
- Won prioritization from the Tier-1 service team.
- Built a dashboard and real-time anomaly alarms after the metrics became available.
- Enabled PM self-service and aimed to provide observability before launching in new locations.

## Reported outcome

The notes say PMs could answer many questions without engineering support and that investigation time fell from days to minutes.

## Conflicting figures

The influence story gives 5–10 days down to 5–10 minutes; the team-goal story describes 3–4 days of manual work per query but does not use the same final timing. Reconcile whether these are different query types or inconsistent drafts before quoting one figure.
