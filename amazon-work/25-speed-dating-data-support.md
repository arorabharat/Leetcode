# Speed-dating date-standardization data support

## Summary

Your team owned a “speed dating” service that translated expiration dates into a standard format based on locale. A customer team urgently needed data about ingested and translated dates.

## Your documented contribution

- Recognized that redirecting the customer team to another service consumer could delay the request.
- Wrote a database-scraping/extraction script to produce the requested ingested and translated date information.
- Delivered the dataset within one day and documented the approach for future requests.

## Outcome

The interview story says the customer received the needed data within 24 hours, faster than multi-team coordination would likely have allowed, and that the approach became reusable.

## Details to verify

The repository does not specify the customer team, data volume, exact schema, privacy controls, or whether the script became a supported tool. The project name and service description are as written in the interview notes.
