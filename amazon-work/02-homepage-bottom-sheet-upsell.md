# Prime homepage bottom-sheet upsell

## Summary

Prime leadership wanted to use an Amazon Pay-owned homepage bottom sheet for Prime upsell offers. The platform had been built around bill-payment reminders, so a Prime subscription flow was not a drop-in fit.

## Problem and constraints

The surface was valuable to both organizations: Prime wanted a new retention/upsell placement, while Amazon Pay wanted to grow usage of its platform. Prime required subscription-specific behavior—benefit and discount messaging, eligibility checks, renewal context, and strict customer-experience expectations—that did not naturally match a billing-reminder surface. Leadership expected quick progress despite these differences.

- Led the technical evaluation from the Prime side.
- Spent about a week documenting Prime requirements, edge cases, and the risks of forcing an integration without redesign.
- Presented the findings to product and engineering leads, helping move the discussion from assumptions to specific technical requirements.
- Aligned responsibilities: your team owned Prime-specific logic and flows; Amazon Pay reviewed compatibility with its platform.

## Outcome

The story says the feature launched and is live on the homepage, showing targeted Prime offers to customers close to expiry or eligible for a promotion.
