# Prime upgrade refunds and the 180-day processor limit

## Summary

You anticipated a customer-support and operations issue when Prime customers upgraded subscription tiers and qualified for prorated refunds, but payment processors could not refund transactions older than 180 days.

## Problem and judgment

Popular content releases were expected to increase upgrades. Long-tenured customers could have prorated refunds blocked by the processor rule, creating failed refunds, manual work, customer friction, and a potential spike in support tickets.

## Your documented contribution

- Evaluated customer impact and operational risk against an already committed project.
- Aligned with your manager to rebalance priorities and delegated part of the project work.
- Built a short-term automation script to identify blocked transactions and prioritize/streamline manual refund handling.
- Partnered with Product on a longer-term alternative compensation mechanism, such as vouchers or Amazon Pay balance, when direct refunds were unavailable.

## Outcome

The story describes reducing anticipated ticket volume and manual effort, but does not give a measured result or state whether the longer-term compensation feature launched.
