# Peak-readiness automation

## Summary

You built a Python tool to automate traffic analysis for peak-readiness preparation, including events such as Prime Day and Black Friday.

## Problem

Teams spent two to three weeks of SDE capacity preparing for peak periods. Traffic analysis alone reportedly took three to four days per team and involved manually collecting current traffic, estimating peak API traffic, gathering client requirements, contacting dependencies, and documenting results.

## Your documented contribution

- Identified traffic analysis as a major and error-prone part of readiness work.
- Built a Python script integrating with a monitoring portal and shipment-forecast APIs.
- Automated analysis for APIs and services and shared the tool with sister teams facing the same process.

## Reported outcome

The story says analysis was reduced from three to four days to about ten minutes, saving engineering time across multiple teams and reducing manual estimation errors. It also says the work received an Innovation Maven award.

## Details to verify

The sources do not identify the exact inputs, forecast model, output/report format, adoption count, or whether the ten-minute result includes dependency coordination and documentation or only traffic analysis.
