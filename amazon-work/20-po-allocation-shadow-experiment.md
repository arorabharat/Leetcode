# PO-allocation algorithm shadow experiment

## Summary

You helped validate a proposed purchase-order allocation algorithm before a broad worldwide rollout by designing and implementing a shadow-mode experiment.

## Problem

The existing PO allocation algorithm was described as static and based on limited signals. Leadership was hesitant to make a large worldwide change without evidence that the proposed multi-signal approach improved allocation confidence.

## Your documented contribution

- Applied learning from the failed PONYS project to advocate validating assumptions before full rollout.
- Proposed a shadow experiment in a small number of fulfillment centers.
- Designed the HLD and LLD for the shadow-mode implementation.
- Worked with an SDE1 on implementation while keeping the experiment from affecting production decisions.
- Collected and analyzed metrics to compare confidence against the existing approach.

## Reported outcome

The experiment reportedly showed at least a 60% improvement in PO-allocation confidence with zero production impact or downtime. The evidence gave leadership confidence to proceed toward a worldwide implementation.

## Details to verify

The repository does not define the confidence metric, sample size, experiment duration, or whether the global rollout completed. The award described in the story is a personal recognition, not a project result.
