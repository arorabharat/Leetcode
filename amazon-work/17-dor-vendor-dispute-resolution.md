# DoR vendor-dispute resolution

## Summary

You led technical direction for an eight-month regulatory project to automatically resolve vendor disputes about inventory received at Amazon fulfillment centers. The notes call it the DoR project; confirm the expanded name before using the acronym externally.

## Problem and scope

EU and French requirements called for inventory information to be surfaced to vendors within 3–10 days of receiving shipments. Vendors could ship 100 units while Amazon recorded 80, creating payment disputes and cash-flow concerns. The effort aimed to scale from pilot vendors to 2,000+ eligible vendors and reduce manual dispute handling.

## Your documented contribution

- Coordinated technical work across eight teams and decomposed work among engineers of different levels.
- Led technical direction for an eight-month delivery, described as February–October 2024.
- Designed a multi-signal resolution approach using OCR, ASN information, and associate inputs, with escalation for uncertain cases.
- Used historical-data simulations and dry runs to validate assumptions before full implementation.
- Organized phased delivery: algorithm validation, core service, shadow integration tests, and graduated production rollout.
- Developed fallbacks when dependencies such as Receive-team signals and TransferX APIs did not work as expected.
- Conducted stakeholder/milestone reviews and maintained the regulatory delivery timeline.

## Reported outcome

The project is described as launching on time, achieving 90% automatic dispute resolution across 2,000+ vendors, and saving approximately $1M per quarter ($10M annualized). A separate passage lists 95% automation as a target, not the achieved result.

## Details to verify

Confirm the meaning of DoR, exact regulatory deadlines, eligible-vendor count, live rollout scope, and the measurement period behind automation/savings. Distinguish achieved 90% from the 95% target.
