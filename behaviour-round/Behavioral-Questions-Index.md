## Atlassian manager-round prompts and follow-ups

[atlassian-round/guidance.md](atlassian-round/guidance.md) is a preparation checklist rather than a verbatim question transcript. It recommends preparing stories for:

- Owning a project end to end and delivering measurable outcomes.
- Improving operational excellence, reliability, performance, or efficiency.
- Leading technical direction and making decisions with ambiguity or trade-offs.
- Creating organizational impact through mentoring, delegation, or knowledge sharing.
- Influencing others, resolving design disagreements, or unblocking teams.
- Adapting when requirements or priorities change.
- Handling a project failure, missed expectations, production issue, or mistake.
- Demonstrating customer impact and cross-team coordination.

The same guide lists these follow-up questions for each story:

- How many engineers and teams were involved?
- What was the system’s scale and complexity?
- How did you estimate the effort and decide milestones?
- What alternatives did you consider, and why did you reject them?
- What went wrong during execution?
- How did you resolve disagreements or unblock dependencies?
- What metrics improved, and by how much?
- What happened after launch?
- What would you do differently now?
